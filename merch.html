Pro Style Poi Grips

These are the finest poi grips on the market - the result of many years of experience and experimentation. Two layers of leather, securely stitched, with an integrated delta quick link and a built-in escape tab for quick release.

These poi handles hang onto you. As you spin, the weight of your props will cinch the loops around your fingers, allowing for an open-palmed style with very delicate control. When you're done with your poi, simply pull on the escape tab and the handles release their grip. This feature is very useful in case of an emergency, should you need to free your hands fast.

The integrated delta quick link has a broad point of attachment to the grips across the full width of the leather straps and connects directly to your chains - simplifying your props and improving your control.

While we would normally recommend you see the quality for yourself... We are offering these grips for sale online, by special request. We're certain that once you try a pair of these grips, you'll be convinced these are the last set of of poi handles you will ever need.
